# Initial Behavioural Taxonomy

This is the initial prototype taxonomy from the mentor plan. It is observation-oriented and must be finalized after literature review and clinical consultation; the student team must not independently invent clinical interpretations.

| Machine key | Human-readable name | Observation-oriented description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `response_to_name` | Response to name | Caregiver observations of how the child responds when their name is called. |
| `eye_contact` | Eye contact | Caregiver observations involving looking toward another person during interaction. |
| `joint_attention` | Joint attention / pointing to share interest | Observations of sharing attention or interest with another person, including pointing to share interest. |
| `gestures` | Gestures | Observations of communicative or social gestures. |
| `social_interaction` | Social interaction / shared enjoyment | Observations of interaction with others or shared enjoyment. |
| `communication_language` | Communication / language | Observations concerning communication or language. |
| `play_pretend_play` | Play and pretend play | Observations of play, including pretend play. |
| `repetitive_behaviour` | Repetitive behaviours | Observations of repetitive behaviours. |
| `restricted_interests` | Restricted interests | Observations of narrow or strongly focused interests. |
| `sensory_behaviour` | Sensory responses | Observations of responses involving sensory experiences. |

The future service must represent status separately from domain and distinguish `not_observed` from a concerning/reduced observation.
